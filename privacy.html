PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Your screenshots are personal. ShotTheName is designed to process them locally on your Mac without sending their contents to our servers.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Privacy by Design

ShotTheName is built around local processing. Screenshot analysis, OCR and file renaming are performed on your Mac using Apple's native frameworks. We do not operate a server that receives your screenshots or extracted screenshot text.

On-Device Processing
No App Telemetry
No Screenshot Uploads

1. Developer Information

ShotTheName is developed and maintained by Meet, an independent developer. For privacy-related questions, requests or feedback, you can contact us using the email address provided at the end of this policy.

2. Local On-Device Processing

ShotTheName uses Apple's native Vision framework to analyze screenshots and recognize text directly on your Mac.

  • Screenshot analysis and OCR are performed locally.
  • Screenshot contents are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Extracted screenshot text is not sent to us.
  • File renaming is performed locally on your device.
  • We do not maintain a cloud database of your screenshots or rename history.

These statements describe the application's screenshot-processing functionality. They do not mean that your Mac or the third-party services you use never communicate over the internet.

3. File Access and Permissions

ShotTheName may request permission to access folders that you choose to monitor or organize.

  • Folder access is used to detect and rename screenshots.
  • File operations take place on your Mac.
  • You control the permissions granted through macOS.
  • We do not receive copies of files accessed by the application.

4. Information Collection

We do not intentionally collect personal information through the ShotTheName application.

  • No user account is required.
  • No registration is required.
  • We do not collect screenshots or extracted OCR content.
  • We do not operate an application analytics or telemetry service.
  • We do not maintain a user database.

5. Website Privacy

Our website, shotthename.xyz, may load resources from third-party services to display its content and functionality.

These resources currently include:

  • Tailwind CSS CDN: Used to load website styling.
  • Google Fonts: Used to load typography.
  • unpkg: Used to load Lucide icons.

When your browser requests resources from these providers, they may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information and request details.

These third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies. We do not control their independent data-handling practices.

6. Homebrew and GitHub Distribution

ShotTheName is distributed through a public GitHub repository and a Homebrew tap.

When you install or update ShotTheName using Homebrew, your device communicates with third-party infrastructure, including GitHub, to retrieve installation files and related metadata.

  • GitHub hosts the application's release files and Homebrew tap.
  • Homebrew manages the installation process on your Mac.
  • These services may process technical connection information according to their own policies.
  • We do not receive your screenshot contents through the installation process.

For more information:

GitHub Privacy Statement Homebrew Official Website

7. Analytics, Tracking and Cookies

We do not intentionally use advertising trackers, behavioral analytics or tracking cookies on our website. The ShotTheName application does not include an application analytics or telemetry service.

However, third-party resources loaded by the website and the website hosting provider may process technical connection information as part of normal service operation.

8. Payments and Purchases

ShotTheName is free to download and use. There are no subscriptions, paid licenses or in-app purchases. We do not collect payment information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ShotTheName evolves or when our services and practices change. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
